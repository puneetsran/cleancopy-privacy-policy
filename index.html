CleanCopy Privacy Policy

CleanCopy does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

All processing—including table extraction, cleaning, and export (CSV, TSV, Markdown, or JSON)—happens entirely on the user’s device.

The extension only accesses webpage content when the user activates it. No data is sent to external servers.

CleanCopy does not track your browsing history or any personally identifiable information. It keeps simple, anonymous usage counts (such as how many times you extract or copy a table) and basic information about how the extension was installed. This is stored only in your browser’s local storage and is never sent anywhere.
